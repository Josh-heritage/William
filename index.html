Biography & Background

William Jackson Heritage joined Torquay Rowing Club with an initial focus on coastal sculling before transitioning into senior multi-crew formations. Based along the South Devon coastline, his training combines endurance work on open waters with high-intensity ergometer conditioning tailored for regional sprint events.

Over past seasons, William has represented the club at prominent South West regattas, securing notable finishes in both flat-water and coastal environments. His current 2026 campaign targets top-three placement in the regional standings alongside squad qualifying runs for upcoming offshore championships.
